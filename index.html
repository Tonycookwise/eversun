04 / Attention at every stage

Quality is in
the details.

From incoming materials to packed products, quality checks are part of the manufacturing process.

IQC / MATERIALS

A considered start.

Incoming materials are checked against the agreed specifications before they enter production.

IPQC / PRODUCTION

Consistency in the making.

In-process checks follow key manufacturing stages to identify issues and keep production aligned.

FQC / BEFORE SHIPMENT

Ready for the next step.

Final inspections cover product function, appearance, packaging and labeling against order requirements.

Compliance & certification documentation

LFGB Food-contact testing
amfori BSCI Social compliance audit

Ask our team for the reports and certificates relevant to your selected products and production site. Applicability, testing conditions, certification scope and validity should be confirmed for each order.
